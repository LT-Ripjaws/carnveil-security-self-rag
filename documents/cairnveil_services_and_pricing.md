# Cairnveil Security - Services and Pricing

This document contains service packaging.

## ShieldGuard MDR

ShieldGuard MDR monitors endpoint, cloud, identity, and SIEM telemetry. It includes alert triage, threat hunting, containment recommendations, and monthly posture reporting.

### ShieldGuard Starter

- Price: USD 4,500 per month.
- Best for teams with up to 250 employees.
- Includes business-hours onboarding, endpoint and identity alert triage, and monthly executive reporting.
- Includes 8 hours of threat hunting per month.
- Does not include 24x7 phone escalation.

### ShieldGuard Pro

- Price: USD 9,800 per month.
- Best for teams with 250 to 1,500 employees.
- Includes 24x7 SOC monitoring, SIEM integration, cloud account monitoring, and identity detection rules.
- Includes 20 hours of threat hunting per month.
- Includes 24x7 phone escalation for severity 1 and severity 2 incidents.
- Includes monthly purple-team validation of the highest-risk detection gaps.

### ShieldGuard Enterprise

- Price: custom annual contract.
- Best for regulated or global organizations.
- Includes all Pro features, dedicated detection engineering, quarterly tabletop exercises, and custom retention options.
- Includes unlimited severity 1 incident triage within the contracted environment.
- Includes customer-specific detection content for cloud, identity, endpoint, and application telemetry.

## BreachReady IR Retainer

BreachReady provides prepaid incident response hours that can be used for compromise assessment, ransomware triage, cloud credential abuse, phishing investigation, and post-incident reporting.

- 40-hour retainer: USD 18,000 per year.
- 80-hour retainer: USD 34,000 per year.
- 160-hour retainer: USD 62,000 per year.
- Unused hours expire at the end of the contract year.
- Retainer customers receive priority scheduling over non-retainer emergency requests.

## CloudTrace CSPM

CloudTrace continuously reviews cloud configuration risks for AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

- Price: USD 1,200 per cloud account per month.
- Includes misconfiguration findings, identity risk findings, exposed storage detection, and remediation guidance.
- Critical cloud exposure findings are escalated through the same severity process as managed detection findings.

## AuditLens Compliance Pack

AuditLens maps collected evidence to SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and PCI DSS control families.

- Price: USD 2,500 per month when added to ShieldGuard Pro or Enterprise.
- Includes evidence export, control mapping, and audit-response summaries.
- AuditLens does not provide legal certification or guarantee audit success.

## Free Trial Policy

ShieldGuard Starter and ShieldGuard Pro include a 14-day trial for qualified prospects. The trial includes limited telemetry onboarding, alert simulation, and a sample executive report. The trial does not include emergency incident response, custom detection engineering, or compliance evidence exports.
