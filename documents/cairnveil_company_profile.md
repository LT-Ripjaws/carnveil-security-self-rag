# Cairnveil Security - Company Profile

Cairnveil Security is a cybersecurity services company. The company helps mid-market and enterprise customers detect, investigate, and contain security incidents across cloud, endpoint, identity, and application environments.

## Mission

Cairnveil Security reduces attacker dwell time by combining managed detection, threat intelligence, incident response, and compliance evidence support. The company prioritizes verified facts, clear containment guidance, and executive-ready incident summaries.

## Operating Model

- The Security Operations Center runs 24x7 from two regions: North America and APAC.
- Every managed customer receives a named Customer Security Lead.
- Critical incidents are handled by an Incident Commander, a Detection Engineer, and a Customer Security Lead.
- Evidence handling follows least-privilege access, audit logging, and customer-approved retention rules.

## Primary Customer Segments

- SaaS companies that need managed detection and response.
- Financial services teams that need identity, cloud, and endpoint monitoring.
- Healthcare technology teams that need HIPAA-aware incident handling.
- E-commerce companies that need PCI DSS evidence support.

## Core Services

- ShieldGuard MDR for managed detection and response.
- CloudTrace CSPM for cloud posture monitoring.
- BreachReady IR Retainer for incident response support.
- PhishProof Awareness for phishing simulation and employee training.
- AuditLens Compliance Pack for SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and PCI evidence mapping.

## Brand Promise

Cairnveil Security does not claim that it can prevent every breach. The company promises fast detection, evidence-backed recommendations, transparent escalation, and practical remediation steps.
